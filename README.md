3. Product Feature Adoption & Engagement Insights
A behavioural analysis exploring how users adopt and engage with product features. Supports product teams in understanding usage patterns and prioritising improvements.

4. Marketing Spend Efficiency & Multi‑Channel Attribution Analysis

A specialist risk‑focused report analysing customer behaviour shocks, operational failures, and market shifts. Includes scenario modelling, sensitivity analysis, and revenue risk forecasting. Designed for leadership, finance teams, and risk managers.
